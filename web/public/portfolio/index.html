Made for group trips

One plan the whole group shares

A Suttru trip belongs to everyone going. Friends, family or colleagues join the same trip, see the same days, routes, bookings and documents, and track one shared budget. Each person gets a role, so everyone can help without anyone breaking the plan.

Create and invite

The organiser creates the trip and invites people by email or with a share link.

Members join

Invitees see the trip on their Invitations page and accept it. It then appears in their My Trips.

Plan together

Planners build the days and pick places. Everyone sees the same itinerary and smart route.

Share the costs

Whoever pays logs the expense. Suttru splits it and shows who owes whom until everyone settles.

Roles decide who can change what

OwnerCreated the trip. Adds and removes members and changes their roles.
EditorCan edit the whole trip: itinerary, places, expenses, bookings and documents.
PlannerPlans the itinerary and saved places.
Expense managerHandles expenses, splits and settlements.
ViewerSees everything in the trip but can't change it.

How a trip comes together

From idea to itinerary in six steps

  1. Create the tripDestination, dates, budget and trip type, then invite your group.
  2. Explore the cityRecommended sights ranked for your trip type, with photos and distances.
  3. Build each dayAssign places to days and add times, notes and estimated costs.
  4. Get the smart routePick your stay and each day is ordered nearest-first on a map.
  5. Split the spendingLog expenses, split them fairly and settle up.
  6. Travel with everythingTickets, documents, flight alerts and offline packs on your phone.
